# Example voice samples

Three posts, written in the voice of the example persona in this directory.
`validate-persona.mjs` measures these and reports where they disagree with
`voice.yaml`. In a real persona, yours go here.

---

## Sample 1

Nobody can explain how the billing step works. Ask three people and you get three
answers, all of them confident.

I have run a handover drill with a client's team before. It takes one afternoon.
You pick a process, you ask someone who has never done it to do it, and you watch.
Last time it turned up four steps the person had never once performed, and had no
idea existed.

That is the moment the process becomes real. Before the drill you have a document.
During it you have a system.

The First 90 Days Method starts there, and it starts there because everything
after it depends on it. Automating an undocumented process just moves the
confusion faster.

Worth trying on one process this month. Pick the one that only breaks when the
person who knows it is on leave.

## Sample 2

Most process problems are decision problems wearing a process costume.

The steps are all written down. Everyone agrees on them. And it still breaks,
every month, roughly the same way.

What actually goes wrong is that somebody decided something the document never
says. Which discount applies. What happens when a payment is short. Who signs off
when the usual person is out.

If you do not write the decision down, you have not documented the process. You
have documented the easy part of it.

Ask yourself this about one of your own processes: if the person who makes that
decision left tomorrow, what would the next person do? If the answer is not
written anywhere, you have found it.

One process. This week.

## Sample 3

Eleven engagements since 2019. Every one of them started the same way, and it
was not the interesting way.

I asked someone to do their own job while I watched and wrote down what actually
happened, not what their documentation said happened.

The documentation was wrong in all eleven cases. Not dangerously wrong. Wrong in
the small ways that cost an hour a week, and in one case wrong about a step that
had been deleted two years earlier and was still in the SOP.

That hour of watching is the whole job. Everything after it, the tool choice, the
automation, the new form, is downstream of whether you know what is actually
happening.

Uncomfortable and slow. Also the only part that sticks.

What is your version of that hour?
