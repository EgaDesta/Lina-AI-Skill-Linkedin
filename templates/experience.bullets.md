# Experience Bullets

`score-profile.mjs` `ps2` requires 60% of bullets in each of the three most
recent roles to contain a digit. That is a floor, not a target. Three bullets per
role, all with numbers, is a good bar.

## Structure

```
{Verb} {what you did}, {how}, {result with number and timeframe}.
```

Not:

- `Responsible for process improvement` — nobody did that at a company
- `Helped with automation initiatives` — helped how?
- `Improved efficiency` — by how much, from what baseline?

## Verb tiers

Weak: supported, assisted, helped with, involved in, participated, contributed to,
worked on, was tasked with, responsible for.

Strong: rebuilt, cut, replaced, removed, automated, consolidated, documented,
designed, shipped, halved, eliminated, migrated, rewrote, instrumented.

Weak verbs are a signal the underlying work was too vague to describe. Usually it
means you have not decided what the result was yet. Fix that before rewriting.

## Thirty result phrases

Cut the time from X to Y. Eliminated the manual step. Reduced errors from N to M.
Wrote the documentation that survived the departure. Took a process nobody could
explain and made it run without a person in it. Merged four tools into one.
Moved N workflows off spreadsheets. Made the audit a five-minute job instead of a
two-day one. Trained the team to run it without me. Shipped the first automated
version. Wrote the checklist that became the standard. Cut onboarding from 14 days
to 3. Automated the handoff that caused most of the complaints. Replaced a
spreadsheet with something that cannot silently break. Documented 60 processes that
had only existed in one person's head. Cut rework by a third. Removed a
dependency on a single vendor. Made the failure mode visible instead of silent.
Reduced the reporting cycle from monthly to weekly. Standardised the input format
so the tool stopped rejecting data. Answered the board question without a
two-week investigation. Turned a complaint into a tracked item. Built the thing
that let someone else do my job while I was away. Reduced exceptions to a set
small enough to review by Friday. Made it safe to fail. Took the project from
undefined scope to a signed-off spec. Removed myself as a dependency. Cut the
approval chain from three steps to one. Wrote it down before I forgot it again.

## Bullet rules

- **One result per bullet.** Two numbers in one bullet means neither is checked.
- **Baseline and result, or neither.** "Cut processing time" is a claim. "Cut it
  from six hours to forty minutes" is evidence.
- **Timeframe.** "In the first quarter" is what makes a number mean anything.
- **Subject matters as much as the number.** "Reduced errors" — whose errors? A
  reader cannot recognise themselves without knowing the situation.
- **No confidential detail.** `proof.policy.require_client_permission`. "A
  logistics client" is safe; the company name is not, unless permission is
  granted.
- **Under 300 characters.** A bullet longer than that is two bullets.

## The weak-profile pattern

```
Weak:
  - Streamlined the onboarding process for new hires
  - Worked with cross-functional teams to improve communication
  - Recognised for strong attention to detail

Strong:
  - Cut onboarding from 14 days to 3 by rebuilding the checklist, the access
    request, and the first-week schedule as three named steps
  - Built the handoff doc 4 managers now use; replaced a meeting that had run
    weekly for two years
  - Instrumented the process so errors surface in week one instead of month two
```

The strong version is specific enough to check, specific enough to steal, and
specific enough that a reader knows whether it applies to them.

## Reverse-derived bullets

The strongest material comes from work nobody wrote down. For each of the three
most recent roles, answer, in one sentence each:

- What was broken, and what did it cost?
- What did you change, concretely?
- What is measurably different now?
- What is still not fixed?

Question four matters. It is what feeds `opinion` and `observation` slots, and it
is the part a competitor cannot copy.
