# Banner Brief

1584 x 396 pixels. Roughly 4:1. It renders as a thin strip on mobile — assume
everything important must survive being 60 pixels tall.

## What it is for

Three jobs, in priority order:

1. Say what this account is about, to someone who has just landed here
2. Carry one contact detail
3. Look like a person designed it on purpose

Not a job: decorate. A banner with a stock photo and no message is worse than a
plain one, because it signals that nothing here needs saying.

## Layout, left to right

```
[left third]    MESSAGEE |ROLE or METHOD
[centre]       (deliberately empty — this is where the face sits)
[right third]  URL or EMAIL
```

The centre gap is not wasted. On desktop the photo overlaps it. A banner with
content in the centre is hidden behind the photo for every visitor.

## Copy rules

- Message: **under 8 words.** No exceptions. At 1584x396 a person reads maybe
  twelve characters at a glance.
- Message must be the pillar promise or the method name, not a slogan.
- Role or method: under 5 words.
- Contact: a short URL or an email. Not a phone number, not three handles.
- No emoji. No stock photography. No gradients with text on them.
- Text contrast must survive the mobile strip. Light text on a dark background,
  never a photograph.

## Deliverable to a designer or an image model

```
size: 1584x396, 4:1
safe zone: keep all text within the left 28% and right 22%; leave the centre
  50% clear of text
background: [single dark or light tone, or a flat brand colour]
text left: "[message, under 8 words]"
text left second line: "[role or method, under 5 words]"
text right: "[short url or email]"
type: [single weight, large, high contrast]
must not contain: faces, stock photography, icons, gradients behind text
alt text: [the full sentence the image replaces]
```

`alt text` is the version of this that always appears. Write it as a sentence,
not a description of the picture.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Stock photo of a handshake | Flat colour. It photographs badly at 4:1 and dates instantly. |
| Text in the centre | Move it. It sits behind the photo. |
| Quotation mark as a design element | Drop it. It was a signature device in 2014. |
| Six-word message | Cut to four. |
| Contact details in the photo area | Never. It changes per session. |
| Emoji as visual interest | Drop it. |
| Banner different from the headline | Make them agree. Two different messages is one too many. |

## The zero-budget version

A solid single colour, with the message on the left in one weight of type, and
the URL on the right. Nothing else. This looks better than almost every banner
made with a template, because the templates are adding clutter to an area nobody
looks at.

## Checking

Not scored by the rubric. `cm1` checks a banner is set at all; nothing here is
machine-measurable. A human looks at it, on mobile, and asks whether the message
is readable in the strip. That is the whole test.
